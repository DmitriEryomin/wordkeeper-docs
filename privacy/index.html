Wordkeeper

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Wordkeeper is a private, local-first dictionary app. This policy explains how the current version of Wordkeeper handles information.

Information Wordkeeper collects

Wordkeeper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or dictionary content. It does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or a remote service to store your dictionary.

Data stored on your device

The words, translations, categories, and app preferences you create are stored locally on your device. Wordkeeper cannot access this information remotely.

Exporting and importing

When you choose to export a backup or printable dictionary, Wordkeeper creates a file on your device and opens the operating system's share sheet. You choose whether and where to share or save that file. The privacy practices of the service or app you choose then apply.

When you choose to import a backup, Wordkeeper reads the file you selected and stores its contents only on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Your dictionary remains on your device until you delete entries, replace the dictionary through an import, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling Wordkeeper may delete its local data. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep it.

Children's privacy

Wordkeeper does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not transmit user data off the device.

Changes to this policy

If Wordkeeper's data practices change, this policy and the app-store privacy disclosures will be updated before or with the relevant app release.
